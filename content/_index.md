I’m a Principal System Integration Engineer. I develop internal services in Go and run them on Kubernetes and virtual machines, looking after their deployment, monitoring, maintenance, and debugging.

I’m also the technical owner of our internal Tailscale network. I design connections between client devices, on-premises infrastructure, and cloud services, with responsibilities spanning network architecture, routing, DNS, and security.

My background is in applied mathematics and scientific computing. Earlier in my career, I worked on numerical methods and mathematical modelling in academic and industrial research. I’ve also been a major contributor to [Gonum](https://gonum.org/), a collection of numerical libraries for Go.
