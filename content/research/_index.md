My background is in applied mathematics and scientific computing. Before moving into my current engineering work, I worked in academic and industrial research in Japan and the Czech Republic.

My work included numerical methods for partial differential equations, multiscale modelling, and applications in materials science, image processing, and biomedical simulation.

## Research record

* [Publications](/publications/) — journal articles, conference contributions, preprints, books, and my PhD thesis.
* [Talks & academic activities](/talks/) — presentations, conference participation, research and study stays, and organising work.

## Research topics

These were the main areas of my earlier research and scientific software work:

* Scientific computing and software development
* Numerical optimization
* Numerical analysis of partial differential equations
* Multi-scale modeling
* Medical image processing
* Computer graphics, physically-based rendering
